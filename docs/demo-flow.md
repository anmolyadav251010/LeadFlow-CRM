# Demo flow walkthrough

This quick walkthrough helps present the app as a finished product during a live demo.

## 1. Sign in

Open the app and sign in with the seeded owner account:

- Email: ava@leadflow.io
- Password: Password123!

This user is assigned to the demo workspace and can access the CRM dashboard.

## 2. Dashboard review

On the dashboard, review the core lead and revenue metrics:

- Total leads in the pipeline
- Qualified lead count
- Open pipeline value
- Conversion rate

Use the cards and charts to explain how sales leaders track health in one place.

## 3. Lead creation

Go to the Leads page and choose New lead. Add a realistic opportunity, including:

- Full name
- Company
- Email and phone
- Lead source
- Status and priority
- Estimated deal value
- Next follow-up date

After saving, the lead appears in the pipeline immediately.

## 4. Follow-up task flow

Create a task from the Tasks page or while reviewing a lead. Add:

- Title and description
- Due date
- Priority
- Status

This demonstrates how the team converts discussions into execution and accountability.

## 5. Reports and account health

Open the Reports page to explain how the team evaluates performance across:

- Qualified rate
- Open pipeline value
- Due tasks
- Closed revenue

This gives a picture of the product’s operational value beyond simple lead storage.

## 6. Contacts and teams

Visit Contacts, Companies, and Teams to show how the CRM scales within a revenue org and supports cross-functional visibility rather than isolated deal tracking.
